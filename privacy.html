Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Data Collection

WatchTower Pro operates entirely within your browser. We collect:

  • License key — stored locally to validate your subscription tier.
  • Monitored ASINs & product data — stored locally in Chrome storage for your monitoring configuration.
  • Email address — collected by Paddle during purchase for license delivery. We do not store this independently.

We do not collect browsing history, personal information, or any data beyond what is necessary for the Extension to function.

2. Data Storage

All monitoring data and settings are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's Storage API. We do not operate any backend servers. No user data is transmitted to us.

3. Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Paddle. We do not collect or store payment information (credit card numbers, etc.). See Paddle's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle your payment data.

4. Third-Party Services

The Extension makes requests to Amazon.com and related domains to fetch product data for monitoring. These requests are made from your browser under your own Amazon session. No data is routed through third-party servers.

5. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at zhoumaocheng@gmail.com.
